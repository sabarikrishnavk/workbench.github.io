ER diagram — types across subgraphs

      
Each box is a type in a subgraph. (extends) boxes are fields a subgraph adds to another subgraph's entity, and (reference) boxes are key-only stubs; both join the owning type by @key. Fields typed with another type become relations (list → one-to-many, nullable → zero-or-one). PK = @key field, FK = field that points at another type.

The full SDL a subgraph contributes for an entity — every @key, type, input, enum, interface and scalar registered under it, plus its Query/Mutation operations. Unlike the Explorer's per-operation Entity SDL (scoped to what one operation reaches), this is the whole set.

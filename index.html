Start here

  1. Open the registry workbench. Click Load .xlsx for your registry, or Load example to explore.
  2. Add the target entities, with domain, business application (subgraph) and LeanIX / Confluence / ER links.
  3. In GQL migration, follow the Onboarding guide: pick the application, its source (MonoGraph, BFF or service REST API) and the operations to migrate.
  4. Edit the generated rows, set them to Proposed, and copy them to the GQL Registry.
  5. Review and approve in the registry, then export or Save & push to SharePoint or Google Drive.
  6. In Code migration, generate the REST/BFF → FedGQL code.

Samples & reference

Retail sample (samples/)

  • Seven subgraphs: product, pnp, inventory, search, cart, payments, checkout. Each folder has the target subgraph.gql and the service's openapi.yaml.
  • mono-graph.gql: the monolith to migrate from.
  • bff-openapi.yaml: composite BFF endpoints to migrate.
  • GQLRegistry-sample.xlsx: the matching registry.
  • DataMappingRegistry-sample.xlsx: product and cart data mapped from MDM / PIM through Kafka, the services and the supergraph to the web, for Data mapping.
